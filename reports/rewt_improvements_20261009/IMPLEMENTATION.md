For a 256-dimensional output vector, centering subtracts the mean of its coordinates from every coordinate. The energy evaluates these centered coordinates, and the output component of its derivative is projected into the same zero-mean subspace. This removes the arbitrary offset that softmax ignores. It does not promise that lower energy will improve task performance.

The repair term is `max(0, CE(final) - stop_gradient(CE(initial)))`, averaged over supervised positions and weighted by 0.1 in the repaired recipes. Here CE is next-byte cross-entropy. Corruption affects 25% of training examples, with output noise standard deviation 0.5 and latent noise 0.05. Those guesses are initializations; all subsequent accepted changes use the fixed total energy. The training loss also retains next-byte prediction, ranking, and applicable forecast/denoising/critic/router losses.

Process feedback is local. A parseable corrupted trace supplies the first wrong intermediate step and the first changed byte in that step. The energy compares correct and wrong byte candidates under their identical preceding prefix. This adds correctness supervision during training without introducing a symbolic solver during inference; it is not a whole-solution verifier or evidence of general reasoning.

The full model retains the forecast, diffusion, specialist, communication, critic and router components. Its frozen forecast teacher is trained from scratch within this course experiment. Language and reasoning use their respective trained teacher copies, and teacher preparation costs are recorded. At a fixed 250K parameter budget, full support modules still compete with feedforward capacity; the report makes that tradeoff explicit.

The comparison declares fresh held-out samples before fitting: nonoverlapping enwik8 windows and text prefixes, plus new synthetic identities that exclude the previous pilot's test identities. Validation screens fourteen cases, selects a repaired recipe, and selects bounded inference policies. Separate equal-time fits include teacher preparation in the full model's cap. Actual generated text and reasoning are saved; a blinded review page leaves human scores pending.

Verification before the main experiment: **39 tests passed**, plus complete small end-to-end training, generation, reporting, and isolated profiling smoke runs. The test suite covers the new geometry, causal caches, fixed inference weights, energy acceptance, corruption, process annotations, filtering and exact optimizer resume. Smoke-run scores are engineering checks, not performance evidence.

Training/source hashes are frozen in [plan.json](/home/kt/Documents/EnergyTransformer/reports/rewt_improvements_20261009/plan.json). The main comparison has 1,048,576 language target bytes per fit, about 17.1 times the first pilot's 61,440 bytes, and 800 reasoning adaptation updates. Final quality and cost conclusions depend on the measured experiment outputs; the implementation changes alone do not establish an improvement.
